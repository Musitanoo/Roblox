PRECANONICAL VISUALIZATION — NOT A 3D OR CANONICAL AUTHORITY

Reconstruct the following gameplay object as a visual design proposal. Do not
invent missing functions, hidden mechanisms, logos, text or additional parts.

ART-DIRECTION PRECEDENCE
- Salvaged Frontier is the sole identity authority.
- Industrial Toy Defense contributes readability discipline only.
- Never create a fifty-fifty hybrid or a literal construction toy.
- Thesis: A warm, intelligent frontier civilization survives by standardizing what must remain dependable and visibly adapting what history has damaged. Every object is a clear tool first, a record of player ingenuity second, and decorative scenery never.
- Standardized chassis share: 75%–85%.
- Authored adaptation share: 15%–25%.
- Detail hierarchy: 72% primary, 23% secondary, 5% tertiary.
- Global asymmetry target: 0.24, maximum 0.36.

OBJECT IDENTITY
- Name: Defense Barricade Small
- Gameplay function: A temporary player-built barrier that delays ground enemies, exposes understandable damage and creates time for the group to adapt its defense.
- One-second read: The player must immediately read a broad grounded friendly barrier, its threat-facing side, its repair access and whether it remains intact, damaged or critical.
- World role: A dependable standardized fortress module carrying one visible field reinforcement that records how its builders adapted a previously weak defensive line.
- Emotional intent: Competent, protective and hopeful under pressure: the barricade should feel maintained and worth repairing, never improvised trash or disposable scenery.
- Dimensions: 8.0 × 4.0 × 2.0 Roblox studs.
- Front: The broad reinforced face points toward incoming enemies.
- Player side: The calmer rear face exposes a protected mint repair status socket.
- Threat side: The threat-facing side presents continuous armor masses and no interaction-colored focal point.
- Collision promise: A simple rectangular gameplay hitbox matches the broad frame and never follows small repair details or displaced cosmetic fragments.
- Expected simultaneous instances: 30

REQUIRED COMPONENTS
- ground_feet: Provide an honest broad footprint and transfer impact into the ground. (fixed)
- standard_frame: Carry the stable family silhouette, dimensions and collision promise. (fixed)
- armor_panels: Form three large readable defensive masses inside the chassis. (destructible)
- repair_brace: Record one purposeful reinforcement of a known weak span. (replaceable)
- repair_socket: Expose the safe-side interaction and state signal without dominating the silhouette. (fixed)

INTERACTION ZONES
- repair_socket at Upper-right area of the rear service face.: Communicate the player-safe repair and inspection point.; cue: A protected recessed socket with a small local mint light and a distinct complete shape.

STATES — ALL DERIVE FROM THE SAME OBJECT
- intact: All three armor masses remain seated; one older copper reinforcement may remain as maintained history. | invariants: footprint, pivot, frame proportions, repair socket location, three-panel identity
- damaged: One threat-facing panel shifts or opens along the declared weak span while the same frame and repair path remain obvious. | invariants: footprint, pivot, frame proportions, repair socket location, component identities
- critical: The same failure chain widens: a second major separation exposes the load path and the structure visibly sags without changing its footprint. | invariants: footprint, pivot, repair socket location, standard frame identity, recognizable original silhouette

OBLIGATIONS
- Preserve a wide, low and grounded defensive silhouette with two visibly load-bearing feet.
- Use three large panel masses within a standardized thick frame.
- Show exactly one dominant reinforcement story classified as reinforcement.
- Keep the rear repair socket protected, local and readable from the elevated gameplay camera.
- Make damage progress through one causal failure chain while preserving the same object.

CONSTRAINTS
- Keep standardized chassis share between seventy-five and eighty-five percent.
- Keep authored adaptation share between fifteen and twenty-five percent.
- Use at most three visible material families and no more than two repair narratives.
- Keep asymmetry near 0.24 and below the constitution maximum of 0.36.
- Maintain function, front direction and state readability at sixty studs and in groups of thirty.

BOUNDED FREEDOMS
- Explore the exact local angle and attachment language of the single copper reinforcement brace.
- Vary controlled panel-edge wear and small fastener groupings without altering silhouette or function.
- Explore subtle value differences among reclaimed composite panels inside the approved palette.

STAGE INSTRUCTION
Produce one clean 2x2 exploration sheet containing four genuinely different silhouette solutions. Each candidate must change at least two primary-mass or negative-space decisions while preserving the same required components, dimensions, front direction and gameplay function. A brace-angle, fastener, wear or trim swap alone is not a different silhouette. Keep the same scale, neutral camera and simplified materials. Do not label the candidates with generated text.

OUTPUT DISCIPLINE
- Stylized, chunky, purposefully repaired and readable on mobile.
- Broad calm planes, explicit load paths, thick joints and protected apertures.
- Deep slate chassis, bone-sand reclaimed composite and bounded sun-copper repair history.
- Mint is local interaction information only; never broad decoration.
- Neutral studio lighting and a neutral background.
- No cinematic depth of field, environmental scene or unrelated props.
- The proposal is visual evidence only and must not imply validated hidden geometry.
