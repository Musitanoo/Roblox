# Art Direction — selected creative contract

## North Star

> **The fortress is the protagonist.**

The player must understand, under mobile pressure:

- what belongs to the fortress;
- what is interactive;
- what is dangerous;
- what caused damage;
- from which direction it arrived;
- which state is intact, damaged, critical, or disabled.

Visual spectacle is subordinate to causality. Effects may amplify an event but
may not obscure target, cause, direction, or priority.

## Selected identity

The founder selected **Salvaged Frontier** as the world identity.

**Industrial Toy Defense** is retained only as execution discipline:

- thick and graspable primary volumes;
- explicit functional construction;
- protected apertures and service points;
- bounded semantic accents;
- modular repetition;
- one-second mobile readability.

This is not a fifty-fifty hybrid. Salvaged Frontier owns world meaning,
materials, history, emotional tone and purposeful asymmetry. When richness and
clarity conflict, the Salvaged meaning is expressed with a simpler and thicker
form.

The complete human-readable contract is
`docs/SALVAGED_FRONTIER_ART_BIBLE.md`. The strict machine source is
`art/selected/salvaged-frontier-constitution.json`.
